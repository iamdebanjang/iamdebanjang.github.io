<h2 style="margin: 80px 0px 10px;"></h2>

I am a doctoral researcher in [Department of Computer Science](https://www.cs.fsu.edu/) at the [Florida State University](https://www.fsu.edu/). 

My research sits in **Multi-modal Learning** at the intersection of **Computer Vision** (CV), **Natural Language Processing** (NLP) with a focus on developing intelligent visual and language systems that are both weakly-supervised and data-efficient. I am particularly interested in areas such as active/incremental learning,  few-shot learning, generative modeling, agentic models, and medical image analysis.

During my doctoral studies, I have been fortunate to gain industry research experience through internships at [Intel Corporation](https://www.intel.com/), [Hewlett Packard Labs](https://www.hpe.com/us/en/hpe-labs/overview.html), and [HP Inc.](https://www.hp.com/). These roles allowed me to work on applied machine learning, trustworthy AI, and large-scale data-driven systems, helping me bridge academic research with practical industrial applications.

I was a recipient of the **Dean's Award for Doctoral Excellence (DADE)** award from the [Department of Computer Science](https://www.cs.fsu.edu/) at the [Florida State University](https://www.fsu.edu/) for outstanding performance as a doctoral student in the department.
